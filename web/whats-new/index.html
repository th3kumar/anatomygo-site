A new AnatomyGo, made for studying

AnatomyGo is a free 3D anatomy atlas for students. No premium tier, no subscription, no ads, and no sign-up to study. The new design gets you from a name to the part you need faster, on a phone or a laptop.

  • Search shows where it sits. Pick the trachea or the heart, and the rest of the body turns to glass around it.
  • One card per structure. About says what it is. Parts lists every landmark with a short description. On a phone, swipe between them.
  • Learn more shows it on its own, with its landmarks on the model and the structures next to it as faint glass.
  • Step through the landmarks with the arrows, or a swipe on a phone.
  • Made for phones too. The whole atlas works on a phone or a tablet, as well as a laptop.
  • Pin, vote and comment in the same card. Add a missing landmark in your name, or copy a link to any structure or landmark. The Playground now lives here, and its old links still work.
