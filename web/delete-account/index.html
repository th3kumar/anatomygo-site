Delete your AnatomyGo account

Your AnatomyGo account is the same on the website and in the AnatomyGo app for Android. Deleting it removes it everywhere. It can't be undone.

In the Android app

  1. Open AnatomyGo and tap About (the ⓘ button at the top right).
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you use AnatomyGo on the website, or no longer have the app, email us from the email address of your Google account and ask us to delete your AnatomyGo account. We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Email a deletion request

mk420464@gmail.com. Please send it from the address you sign in with, so we know the request is yours.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in: the link to your Google account, your email address, name and photo
  • Your public name
  • Your votes, comments and reports

What is kept

  • Pins you placed stay in the atlas for other learners, without your name.
  • A record that an account was deleted, by its internal number only, with no name or email address.
  • Usage statistics aren't linked to your account. Google Analytics deletes them within 14 months.

Feedback you sent isn't part of your account. If you'd like that deleted too, say so in your email.

Only want to stop?

You can sign out in About → Account instead. To stop usage statistics, turn off Share usage statistics in About. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. More in our privacy policy.
