Privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026

AnatomyGo is a free 3D anatomy atlas for students, on the website anatomygo.in and in the AnatomyGo app for Android. It is run by an independent developer, “we” below. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

  • No ads, and we never sell your data. Nothing is used for advertising or shared with advertisers.
  • Studying on the website needs no account. The Android app asks you to sign in with Google, so your pins, votes and comments are saved to your account.
  • What you publish is public. Pins and comments show your public name (your Google first name, which you can change).
  • Usage statistics can be turned off in About, on the website and in the app.
  • You can delete your account at any time. See Delete your account.

What we collect

Your account. When you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address, profile photo and account ID with us. We use them to create your account and keep you signed in. Your email address and photo are never shown to other people.

Your public name. The first time you save a pin or post a comment, your Google first name becomes your public name, shown next to what you publish. You can change it in About → Account.

What you contribute. Pins you place (where on the model, the name and the description you write), your votes, your comments and any reports you send. Pins and comments are public, with your public name. Vote totals are public, but not who voted. Reports are seen only by us.

Feedback. If you send feedback, we store your message and, only if you type it, an email address so we can reply.

Searches you ask us to add. When a website search finds nothing and you choose “Ask for it”, we store the search words (lowercase letters only) and whether you used a phone or a computer. They are not linked to your account.

Usage statistics (on unless you turn them off). Google Analytics on the website, and Firebase Analytics in the app, record the pages and features used, load times, the type of device and browser, and an approximate location (country and city) that Google derives from your connection. On the website, a search sends only its anatomy words, plus the words of a search that finds nothing. The app sends no search text. Statistics never include your pins, notes, comments or anything else you type. Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off.

Crash reports (Android app, same switch). If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends technical details: what failed, the device model and the Android version.

What the app needs to run. The app downloads the 3D body from jsDelivr and GitHub. It checks Google Play for updates. Through Firebase Remote Config it fetches settings such as how updates are offered, which sends a Firebase installation ID, the app version and the device language. It sends a notification only if you ask it to tell you when the body is ready.

On your device only. Your settings, theme, drafts and the downloaded model stay in your browser's storage or the app's storage, and are not sent to us.

Server logs. Like any website, our hosts (GitHub Pages for the website, Supabase for accounts and pins) see your IP address and browser details when you connect, and keep them briefly in their logs.

Why we use it

  • To run AnatomyGo: sign you in, save and show what you contribute, and keep the atlas working offline.
  • To keep the community atlas accurate and safe: review pins, act on reports and stop abuse.
  • To improve AnatomyGo: find what is slow, broken or missing, using usage statistics, crash reports, feedback and “Ask for it” searches.

We process your account and contributions to provide the service you sign up for, with your consent. You can withdraw that consent by deleting your account.

Who processes it for us

GoogleSign-in, Google Analytics and Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config), and Google Play updates
SupabaseOur database: accounts, public names, pins, votes, comments, reports, feedback and “Ask for it” searches
GitHub and jsDelivrHosting the website and delivering the 3D model files

They process data only to provide these services to us, under their own security and privacy terms. Their servers may be outside India. We don't sell your data or share it with anyone for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and what you contribute: until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your sign-in, email address, public name, votes, comments and reports. Pins you placed stay in the atlas for other learners, without your name.
  • Feedback and “Ask for it” searches: as long as they help us improve AnatomyGo. Ask us and we'll delete your feedback.
  • Usage statistics: Google Analytics keeps event-level data for no longer than 14 months.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • see a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct it: change your public name in About → Account, or ask us to;
  • erase it: delete your account in the app, or ask us (see Delete your account);
  • withdraw consent, by deleting your account or turning off usage statistics;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to;
  • raise a grievance with us, and if we don't resolve it, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Email us from the address of your Google account so we can confirm it is you. We aim to reply within 30 days.

Children

Anyone can study on the website without an account. Signing in and contributing are meant for people aged 18 or over, or younger with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you think a child has signed in without that permission, tell us and we'll delete the account.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections. In the app, your sign-in is stored encrypted with the phone's own key store. Only we can read private data such as email addresses, feedback and reports.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If a change affects how we use data you have already given us, we'll say so on the website and in the app before it applies.
